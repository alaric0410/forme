隐私声明

FORME 隐私声明　更新日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日

简单说：FORME 不收集你的个人信息。照片、视频和设置都只存在你自己的手机里，开发者看不到。

一、不用注册，也不追踪

FORME 不需要账号，不收集姓名、电话、位置或通讯录。没有广告，没有统计分析，也不会跨 App 追踪你。

二、用到的权限

  • 相机：实时取景和拍照。
  • 麦克风：拍实况照片时，录下按快门前后的声音。不拍实况就不会用到。
  • 照片：把拍好的照片存进系统相册；读取 FORME 自己存进去的照片（取回原图、整理空间）。只有你选择浏览系统图库时，才会读取其他照片。
  • 运动感应：显示水平仪，感知“摇晃显影”。数据当场用完，不保存。
  • 位置（仅在使用 App 时）：拍照时把拍摄地点写进照片，方便在相册和明信片里看到在哪拍的。位置只存在照片里，不会发给开发者；可以在 设置 › 记录拍摄地点 关掉。

这些都只在手机上处理，你随时可以在系统“设置”里关掉。

三、照片存在哪里

照片存在 FORME 在你手机里的空间，以及你保存到的系统相册。照片自带的拍摄时间、相机参数和地点，只用来在 App 里显示。删除 FORME，它自己存的照片和设置会一起删除；已经存进系统相册的不受影响。

如果你开了 iCloud 照片，系统可能会从 iCloud 下载你要看的照片。这一步由苹果完成，适用苹果的隐私政策。

在明信片或邮票里选择带上地点时，FORME 会把这张照片的拍摄坐标交给苹果的地图服务换成地名；不选就不会。

四、联系开发者

只有你在“联系开发者”页面主动点“发送”时，开发者才会收到：你写的内容、你选填的邮箱，以及（如果你勾选）机型、系统版本和 App 版本。消息经第三方表单服务 Web3Forms 转成邮件发给开发者，只用来回复你和改进 FORME，不会出售，也不会用于其他用途。邮箱可以不填。

五、儿童

FORME 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

六、声明有变化时

如果这份声明有重要变化，FORME 会在你下次打开时再请你确认。

七、联系我们

在 设置 › 联系开发者 写信给我们，或发邮件到 yijianjin24@gmail.com。

Privacy Statement

FORME Privacy Statement　Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: FORME does not collect your personal information. Your photos, videos and settings stay on your own phone, and the developer cannot see them.

No sign-up, no tracking

FORME needs no account. It does not collect your name, phone number, location or contacts. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking across apps.

Permissions it uses

  • Camera: the live viewfinder and taking photos.
  • Microphone: the sound just before and after the shutter, for Live Photos only.
  • Photos: saving your shots to your photo library, and reading the photos FORME saved there (to fetch originals and free up space). Other photos are read only when you choose to browse your library.
  • Motion: the level in the viewfinder and “Shake to develop”. The data is used on the spot and never stored.
  • Location (only while you use the app): where you take a photo is written into the photo, so you can see the place in your library and on postcards. It stays in the photo and is never sent to the developer; turn it off in Settings › Record location.

All of this stays on your phone, and you can turn any of it off in the Settings app.

Where your photos live

Photos are kept in FORME’s own space on your phone and in the photo library you save them to. The time, camera details and place that a photo already carries are only shown inside the app. Deleting FORME deletes its own photos and settings; anything saved to your photo library stays.

If iCloud Photos is on, the system may download a photo from iCloud when you open it. Apple does that, under Apple’s privacy policy.

If you choose to add the place to a postcard or stamp, FORME asks Apple’s map service to turn the photo’s coordinates into a place name. Nothing is sent if you don’t.

Contacting the developer

Only when you tap “Send” on the Contact page does the developer receive anything: what you wrote, the email address you chose to give, and (if you leave it ticked) your phone model, system version and app version. A third-party form service, Web3Forms, turns the message into an email to the developer. It is used only to reply to you and to improve FORME, and is never sold or used for anything else. The email address is optional.

Children

FORME collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this statement changes in an important way, FORME will ask you to agree again the next time you open it.

Contact

Write to us from Settings › Contact the developer, or email yijianjin24@gmail.com.
